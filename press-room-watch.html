But the recurring narrative register shows where scrutiny persists: Champions League qualification, individual midfield roles, officials, tactical flexibility and whether the squad rebuild has gone far enough.

04 · THE METHOD

Count first. Interpret second.

Every public finding can be traced back to preserved wording. The labels describe observable language and published answers—not a journalist’s private motive or Carrick’s private intent.

One journalist turn, one primary topic.

A compound question still counts as one turn. It may carry one secondary topic, but secondary labels never inflate the headline ranking.
