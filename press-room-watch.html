05 · THE METHOD

Count first. Interpret second.

Every public finding can be traced back to preserved wording. The labels describe observable language and published answers—not a journalist’s private motive or Carrick’s private intent.

One journalist turn, one primary topic.

A compound question still counts as one turn. It may carry one secondary topic, but secondary labels never inflate the headline ranking.

Wording is preserved, not paraphrased.

Exact wording is retained wherever possible. Where the club publishes an answer without the question, the response is preserved but no question is invented. Auto-caption wording is marked transcript-derived; wording published by the club is marked article-verbatim. Missing publications are recorded as gaps — never treated as proof that no conference or question occurred.

Framing is about wording, not motive.

Critical, premise-led and confrontational labels describe the visible construction of a question. Allegations remain attributed, and disputed premises are not converted into facts. Questioner and outlet remain unconfirmed unless identified by the footage, moderator or another reliable source.
