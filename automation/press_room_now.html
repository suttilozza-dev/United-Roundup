Fitness was the largest cluster at nine, 59 all-time; performance and standards, the biggest theme over the season, matched leadership on eight. The newer feature is hierarchy: seven of the nine questions on that theme in the tracker were asked in these three conferences, on Lisandro Martínez’s comments, Jason Wilcox, Erik ten Hag’s account of the club and whether Carrick feels backed. Fourteen of the 50 were premise-led, several resting on someone else’s words: Wayne Rooney on Bruno Fernandes, Nicky Butt on Carrick, ten Hag on culture and promises. The tracker records what was asked, not whether those claims are right. Carrick’s answers stayed level. After the 1–1 he called the performance “a bit of a fresh start”. (All Confirmed, from the club’s own articles.) Opinion: the tracker does not show another theme replacing the results question; it shows more themes running alongside it.
