UNITED ROUNDUP’S READ

The questions have moved from the job to the referee. Mauricio Pochettino’s count starts with his first United press conference, before Liverpool on 20 November. The three conferences so far produced 31 questions: 10 on appointment and role, and 4 each on performance and standards, pressure and mentality, and officials and decisions. Nine of the appointment questions came on the first day. After the 4–0 defeat the questions were about the goals conceded and the red card. At the Sporting CP pre-match, three of the nine were about the Yoro red card and VAR, and he did not leave it alone (Attributed, Pochettino in the club’s Q&A). Opinion: too early to call a pattern, but the referee is the one topic that has grown at each conference. The United Roundup view: a new head coach arguing about officials in his first week is a choice, and the first thing to watch is whether the reporters keep asking him to.
