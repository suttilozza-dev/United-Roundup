UNITED ROUNDUP’S READ

The referee is now as big a story as the defending. Mauricio Pochettino’s count starts with his first United press conference, before Liverpool on 20 November. The four conferences so far produced 40 questions: 11 on appointment and role, 7 on performance and standards, 7 on officials and decisions, and 4 each on pressure and mentality and on tactics and selection. Nine of the appointment questions came on the first day, and the rest have been one at a time. Officials went from none, to one, to three before Sporting and three after it. At the post-match the first two questions on the Tielemans foul got no spoken answer in the club’s Q&A, only a gesture, and he returned to the point in a later answer (Attributed, Pochettino in the club’s Q&A). Opinion: two conferences in a row with three officials questions each is the start of a pattern, not yet one. The United Roundup view: the questions are following the matches, and the first Old Trafford conference, before Brentford, will show whether the referees are still what the reporters want to ask about.
